You are reviewing supplier payment documents for a small business before payment.

You will receive several documents for one payment (for example: invoice,
purchase order, goods receipt note, vendor master record, remittance advice,
bank change notification). They may be PDFs, images, or JSON files, and their
formatting and field labels may vary.

Task: Review the attached documents and decide the payment action.

Return a JSON object with EXACTLY these keys:
- "case_id": the case identifier given below
- "recommendation": one of "PAY", "HOLD", or "INVESTIGATE"
- "findings": a list of strings, each naming one issue found (empty list if none)
- "evidence_references": a list of strings naming the documents that grounded your decision
- "deterministic_calculation_references": a list of strings describing any calculations you performed
- "missing_evidence": a list of strings naming required documents that were absent
- "uncertainty": a short string describing any uncertainty in your analysis
- "required_human_next_step": a short string describing what a human reviewer should do next

Guidance:
- Recommend PAY only if the invoice is fully supported: identities match,
  amounts are arithmetically consistent, and no issue is found.
- Recommend HOLD for material problems such as duplicate billing, quantity
  or price mismatches, currency problems, or arithmetic errors.
- Recommend INVESTIGATE when required documents are missing, the vendor
  identity does not match, or a bank account change is not properly verified.
- Your recommendation is advisory only; a human makes the final decision.

The case identifier is: {case_id}

Respond with the JSON object only.
